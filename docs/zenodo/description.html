Data for the manuscript Which pLM to choose? (preprint: doi:10.1101/2025.10.30.685515; code: github.com/tsenoner/plm_choice).

The study compares 14 protein language models (pLMs) and a random Gaussian control on 75,849,972 Swiss-Prot protein pairs, each scored for sequence identity (PIDE), HFSP and structural similarity (TM-score). It separates the signal already in raw embedding distances from the signal a supervised probe extracts, and compares embeddings with an MMseqs2 homology search for transferring EC numbers and GO molecular-function terms.

This record holds:

  • per-protein embeddings of 540,881 Swiss-Prot 2024_01 proteins for all 15 embedding sets (embeddings_*.h5);
  • the deduplicated pair set with its training, validation and test splits, pair counts and an identical-sequence mask;
  • the probe grids (trained probes and raw-distance scores for 3 targets) on native embeddings, 128-dimensional PCA projections and 11 untrained networks;
  • the EC and GO-MF cohorts with their annotation-transfer results and the MMseqs2 homology baseline;
  • test pairs rescored from experimental PDB structures;
  • per-model distance histograms, and 5,000,000 random pairs with their distances under every model;
  • the New2024 test set of proteins new in UniRef50 2025_01.
